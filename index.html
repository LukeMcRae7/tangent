Features

Build your ideas
without friction.

01

Exact, not approximate

Modelling happens on a boundary representation.

OpenCASCADE B-rep · STEP in and out

02

Holes chosen by the screw

Pick M2 to M10 and a fit. The millimetres come from the standards, with the allowance a nozzle takes back.

Close · normal · loose · tapped · counterbore · countersink

03

Threads made easy

The helix is a preset geometry, not a texture. A printed part has no second operation to cut it with.

Inside and outside · printed fit by default

04

Rounds and chamfers

Round every rim of a part in one operation, and chamfer the bottom edge against elephant's foot.

Per-edge radii · tapered rounds · chamfers

05

Checked before it leaves

Open surfaces and non-manifold edges are refused, with the reason, before a slicer ever sees them.

3MF · STL · tessellated to your tolerance

And the rest of the bench

  • Drawings as sketches

    Import an SVG at its real size, then extrude the letters, not the holes in them.

  • Splits with pins

    Cut a part too big for the bed, with alignment pins and sockets cut for a printed fit.

  • Offset

    Grow or shrink a whole body with its corners kept, for a clearance copy or a pocket.

  • Delete face

    Remove a hole or a boss from a STEP file, and the surrounding faces close over the gap.

  • Push and pull

    Drag any face, including curved ones, to make a collar or cut a groove.

  • Assemblies

    Joint parts together, check the clearances between them, and section through the whole.
